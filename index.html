Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question below to see the answer.

1. What should I wear on my first day and throughout the week?
Our Singapore office follows a Smart Casual dress code from Monday to Thursday, and a relaxed Casual Friday. Clean sneakers, polo shirts, dresses, trousers, and chinos are all great. We keep the air conditioning brisk, so having a light cardigan or jacket at your desk is recommended!
2. Where can I store my lunch or find food nearby?
Our Level 18 pantry is equipped with shared clean refrigerators, microwaves, and dining tables. If you prefer to eat out, there are dozens of options within a 3-minute sheltered walk, including Marina Bay Link Mall food court, Lau Pa Sat, and local hawker stalls around Shenton Way.
3. How and when do I get my permanent building access pass?
On Day 1, you will use a temporary visitor lanyard provided at the security desk. During your HR orientation, we will capture your badge photo, and your permanent RFID office card will be activated and ready for collection by Tuesday afternoon at Reception.
4. When is monthly payroll processed in Singapore?
Salaries are credited on the 20th of each month via direct bank transfer (or the preceding working day if the 20th falls on a weekend or public holiday). Please ensure your bank details and CPF registration are filled in on the Employee Portal by Thursday of your first week.
5. What should I do if I feel unwell during my first week?
Your well-being is our highest priority! If you feel sick, please send a quick message to your direct manager and HR via email or Slack. Our company medical insurance card is active from your first day, allowing cashless visits at over 500 panel clinics across Singapore.
